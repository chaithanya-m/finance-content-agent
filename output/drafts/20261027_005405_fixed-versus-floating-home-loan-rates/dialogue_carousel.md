## Slide 2 — SCENARIO

**Title:** Imagine a Piggy Bank with Different Rates

**👩 Riya (Guide):** Riya: Sure, Arjun! Imagine you have a piggy bank where you put your allowance. Now, if you put your money in a piggy bank that doesn't change, that's like a fixed-rate home loan. ## Slide 3 — EXPLANATION

**Title:** Fixed vs. Floating Home Loan Rates Explained

**👩 Riya (Guide):** Riya: With a fixed-rate home loan, the interest rate stays the same throughout the loan period. This means you'll pay the same amount each month, which can be easier to budget for. But if interest rates go up, you might have to pay more money than you planned.

**📱 On-screen text:** Riya: "Riya: With a fixed-rate home loan, the interest rate stays the same throughout the loan period. This means you'll pay the same amount each month, which can be easier to budget for. But if interest rates go up, you might have to pay more money than you planned."

**🎨 Visual prompt:** Riya (GUIDE) with a friendly smile, pointing to a simple diagram or infographic that illustrates the concept. Clean layout, no clutter. Key term highlighted in a coloured box.

**📌 Verified fact used:** UCBs must check borrowers' repayment capacity for floating-rate housing loans, including possible EMI increases when benchmark rates rise.

## Slide 4 — EXAMPLE

**Title:** Example of How Interest Rates Can Change

**👩 Riya (Guide):** Riya: Let's say you take a fixed-rate home loan of Rs. 50 lakh at an annual interest rate of 8%. If the interest rate goes up to 9% after 5 years, you'll have to pay more money each month to keep up with the higher interest. That's why it's important to check your repayment capacity and understand how floating-rate loans work.

**📱 On-screen text:** Riya: "Riya: Let's say you take a fixed-rate home loan of Rs. 50 lakh at an annual interest rate of 8%. If the interest rate goes up to 9% after 5 years, you'll have to pay more money each month to keep up with the higher interest. That's why it's important to check your repayment capacity and understand how floating-rate loans work."

**🎨 Visual prompt:** Clean worked-example card with simple numbers and a visual calculator or table. Both characters react — Arjun has an 'aha!' expression. Numbers are large and readable.

**📌 Verified fact used:** UCBs are advised to have a policy for resetting floating rates on EMI-based housing loans.
