**👩 Riya [CONSEQUENCES]:** Riya: If you don't understand your credit score, it can be hard for you to get loans or credit cards. It's like if you didn't know how to read your scorecard, you might not know if you're a good borrower or not. So it's important to understand your credit score and how it affects your financial future.

**👩 Riya [TAKEAWAY]:** Riya: One thing you can do today is check your credit score. You can do this for free online or at a bank. It's a good idea to know how much you owe and what your credit score is before you apply for a loan or credit card.

## Verified facts (do not change or add to these)

- A credit score is a three-digit number that summarises your credit history and indicates the likelihood that you will repay a loan on time.
- Banks and financial institutions use credit scores to assess the creditworthiness of applicants before approving loans or credit cards.
- Timely repayment of EMIs and credit card dues is one of the most important factors that helps build and maintain a healthy credit score.
