## Full Teaching Dialogue

*(Use this to understand the conversational flow before rendering scenes.)*

**🧑 Arjun [HOOK]:** Arjun: Hey Riya, I've been saving some money for college, but I'm not sure how it's going to grow. Can you explain compounding to me?

**👩 Riya [SCENARIO]:** Riya: Sure, Arjun! Imagine you have a piggy bank where you put your coins. Now, let's say you put in $100 and every year, the bank gives you 5% interest on your money. At the end of the first year, you'll have $105. But here's the cool part - the bank also gives you interest on that $5! So by the end of the second year, you'll have $110.50. And the next year, you'll get interest on that $110.50, making it even more money!

**👩 Riya [EXPLANATION]:** Riya: Compounding is like magic! It's when you earn interest not just on the money you put in at the start, but also on the interest you've already earned. It's like getting a little bonus for every little bit you save!

**👩 Riya [EXAMPLE]:** Riya: Let's say you save $100 a year for 10 years at a 5% interest rate. By the end of 10 years, you'll have $1,628.89! That's a lot more than if you only saved $100 a year without any interest. It's like getting a free ride on the money train!

**👩 Riya [CONSEQUENCES]:** Riya: If you don't understand compounding, you might miss out on a lot of money. It's like not taking the time to grow your piggy bank. But if you do understand it, you can save more money and have more money to spend on things you want!

**👩 Riya [TAKEAWAY]:** Riya: The next time you're saving money, remember to think about compounding. It can make your money grow faster and help you reach your goals!
