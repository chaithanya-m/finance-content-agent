# What is the RBI repo rate and how does it affect borrowers?

## Hook

What is the RBI repo rate and how does it affect borrowers?

## Slides

### Slide 1

At the time of sanction, REs shall clearly communicate to the borrowers about the possible impact of change in benchmark interest rate on the loan leading to changes in EMI and/or tenor or both.

### Slide 2

REs shall share / make accessible to the borrowers, through appropriate channels, a statement at the end of each quarter which shall at the minimum, enumerate the principal and interest recovered till date, EMI amount, number of EMIs left and annualized rate of interest / Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for the entire tenor of the loan.

### Slide 3

Policy Repo Rate : 5.50%

### Slide 4

Fixed Reverse Repo Rate : 3.35%
