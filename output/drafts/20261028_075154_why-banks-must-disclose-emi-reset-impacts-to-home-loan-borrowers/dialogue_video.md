## Full Teaching Dialogue

*(Use this to understand the conversational flow before rendering scenes.)*

**🧑 Arjun [HOOK]:** Arjun: Hey Riya, what's the deal with banks and EMI resets on home loans?

**👩 Riya [SCENARIO]:** Riya: Well, Arjun, imagine you have a big piggy bank for your house. Now, imagine the bank keeps changing the rules about how much money you need to put in each week. That's kind of like what happens with your home loan EMI.

**👩 Riya [EXPLANATION]:** Riya: When the bank decides to change the rules (that's what we call a 'reset'), it's like they're saying you need to put in more money each week to keep your house. It's important to know this because it can affect how much you can afford to spend on other things.

**👩 Riya [EXAMPLE]:** Riya: Let's say you have a home loan with an EMI of $1,000 per month. If the bank decides to increase the EMI, it might go up to $1,200 per month. That's a big difference! Now, you might have to cut back on other expenses to stay on top of your payments.

**👩 Riya [CONSEQUENCES]:** Riya: If you don't understand this, it can be like not knowing when to put more money in your piggy bank. It can make it harder to save for other things you want, like a new bike or a trip to the beach.

**👩 Riya [TAKEAWAY]:** Riya: The best thing you can do is to ask your bank about EMI resets and how they affect your payments. It's like asking your parents about when you need to put more money in your piggy bank.

## Verified facts (do not change or add to these)

- UCBs must check borrowers' repayment capacity for floating-rate housing loans, including possible EMI increases when benchmark rates rise.
- UCBs are advised to have a policy for resetting floating rates on EMI-based housing loans.
