## Full Teaching Dialogue

*(Use this to understand the conversational flow before rendering scenes.)*

**🧑 Arjun [HOOK]:** Arjun: Hey Riya, why do banks change their interest rates? It's like magic, isn't it?

**👩 Riya [SCENARIO]:** Riya: Yes, Arjun! Let's imagine you have a piggy bank. Banks are like piggy banks for grown-ups. They keep money in them to lend to people who need loans to buy houses, cars, or start businesses.

**👩 Riya [EXPLANATION]:** Riya: Now, imagine if you put your piggy bank in a place where it gets very hot, and it gets really hot inside. That's kind of like what happens to money in banks. When the RBI (that's the bank of the country) raises interest rates, it's like putting your piggy bank in a hot place. This makes it harder for people to borrow money, so banks get more money to lend.

**👩 Riya [EXAMPLE]:** Riya: Let's say the RBI raises the interest rate from 5.50% to 6.00%. That means if you want to borrow money from a bank, you'll have to pay more interest. For example, if you borrow ₹10,000, you'll have to pay ₹550 in interest if the interest rate is 5.50%, but if it's 6.00%, you'll have to pay ₹600.

**👩 Riya [CONSEQUENCES]:** Riya: If you don't understand why banks change interest rates, it can be like not knowing how to use your piggy bank properly. You might end up with less money than you need to buy what you want, or you might not be able to borrow money when you need it. It's like not knowing how to use your piggy bank at all!

**👩 Riya [TAKEAWAY]:** Riya: So, the next time you hear about banks changing interest rates, remember that it's like the RBI is making it harder or easier for people to borrow money. It's important to understand this so you can make smart decisions with your money.
