## Full Teaching Dialogue

*(Use this to understand the conversational flow before rendering scenes.)*

**🧑 Arjun [HOOK]:** Arjun: Hey, Riya! Can you explain why some loans have a longer tenure? I'm a bit confused about that.

**👩 Riya [SCENARIO]:** Riya: Sure, Arjun! Imagine you have a piggy bank, and you save money in it. Now, if you save for a long time, your piggy bank will grow bigger because the money you save also earns interest. This is called compounding.

**👩 Riya [EXPLANATION]:** Riya: It's like magic! Compounding means that your money grows not just by adding more money, but also by adding the interest you earn on that money. It's like getting a bonus for saving your savings!

**👩 Riya [EXAMPLE]:** Riya: Let's say you save $100 in your piggy bank every month for 10 years. If you earn 5% interest each year, by the end of 10 years, your piggy bank will have grown to about $1,790! That's because the interest you earn each year is added to your savings, and then the next year you earn interest on that new amount.

**👩 Riya [CONSEQUENCES]:** Riya: If you don't understand how compounding works, it can be like not knowing how to use a magic wand to make your savings grow. It might take longer for your piggy bank to grow, and you might not have enough money to buy what you want when you need it.

**👩 Riya [TAKEAWAY]:** Riya: The best way to understand compounding is to start saving early. The more time you have to let your money grow, the more it can grow! So, if you can start saving now, you'll have more money to buy things later.

## Verified facts (do not change or add to these)

- The power of compounding means that your investment earns returns not only on the principal but also on the accumulated interest over time.
- Starting to invest early allows more time for compounding to work, significantly increasing the final corpus compared to starting later.
- Compound interest is calculated on the initial principal and the interest accumulated over previous periods of a deposit or loan.
