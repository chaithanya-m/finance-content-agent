## Full Teaching Dialogue

*(Use this to understand the conversational flow before rendering scenes.)*

**🧑 Arjun [HOOK]:** Riya didi, I saw in the news that RBI changed the repo rate to 5.50%. What even is a repo rate, and does it affect my parents' home loan EMI?

**👩 Riya [SCENARIO]:** Great question, Arjun! Your parents took a home loan at a floating rate. That means whenever RBI changes a key rate called the 'repo rate', their monthly EMI can go up or down — sometimes without any notice.

**👩 Riya [EXPLANATION]:** The repo rate is the rate at which RBI lends money to banks. When RBI sets it at 5.50%, it becomes cheaper or costlier for banks to borrow. Banks then adjust your home loan interest rate accordingly — that's what makes it 'floating'.

**👩 Riya [EXAMPLE]:** Say the repo rate is 5.50% today. If RBI increases it next quarter, your bank may raise your home loan rate. As per RBI rules, your bank must inform you at sanction about these possible changes — and send you a quarterly statement showing your remaining EMIs.

**👩 Riya [CONSEQUENCES]:** If you don't know about this, a sudden EMI increase feels like a shock. But here's the good news: RBI now mandates that your bank must clearly communicate any rate-change impact at the time they give you the loan — so you can plan ahead.

**👩 Riya [TAKEAWAY]:** If your family has a floating-rate home loan, ask your bank for the quarterly statement that shows the current rate, EMIs paid, and EMIs remaining. This is your right under RBI rules — and it helps you plan your budget better.

## Verified facts (do not change or add to these)

- At the time of sanction, REs shall clearly communicate to the borrowers about the possible impact of change in benchmark interest rate on the loan leading to changes in EMI and/or tenor or both.
- REs shall share / make accessible to the borrowers, through appropriate channels, a statement at the end of each quarter which shall at the minimum, enumerate the principal and interest recovered till date, EMI amount, number of EMIs left and annualized rate of interest / Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for the entire tenor of the loan.
- Policy Repo Rate : 5.50%
- Fixed Reverse Repo Rate : 3.35%
- Policy Rates Policy Repo Rate : 5.50%
