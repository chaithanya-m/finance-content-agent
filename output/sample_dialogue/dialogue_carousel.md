## Slide 3 — EXPLANATION

**Title:** What exactly IS the repo rate?

**👩 Riya (Guide):** The repo rate is the rate at which RBI lends money to banks. When RBI sets it at 5.50%, it becomes cheaper or costlier for banks to borrow. Banks then adjust your home loan interest rate accordingly — that's what makes it 'floating'.

**📱 On-screen text:** Riya: "The repo rate is the rate at which RBI lends money to banks. When RBI sets it at 5.50%, it becomes cheaper or costlier for banks to borrow. Banks then adjust your home loan interest rate accordingly — that's what makes it 'floating'."

**🎨 Visual prompt:** Riya (GUIDE) with a friendly smile, pointing to a simple diagram or infographic that illustrates the concept. Clean layout, no clutter. Key term highlighted in a coloured box.

**📌 Verified fact used:** At the time of sanction, REs shall clearly communicate to the borrowers about the possible impact of change in benchmark interest rate on the loan leading to changes in EMI and/or tenor or both.

## Slide 4 — EXAMPLE

**Title:** How it actually works

**👩 Riya (Guide):** Say the repo rate is 5.50% today. If RBI increases it next quarter, your bank may raise your home loan rate. As per RBI rules, your bank must inform you at sanction about these possible changes — and send you a quarterly statement showing your remaining EMIs.

**📱 On-screen text:** Riya: "Say the repo rate is 5.50% today. If RBI increases it next quarter, your bank may raise your home loan rate. As per RBI rules, your bank must inform you at sanction about these possible changes — and send you a quarterly statement showing your remaining EMIs."

**🎨 Visual prompt:** Clean worked-example card with simple numbers and a visual calculator or table. Both characters react — Arjun has an 'aha!' expression. Numbers are large and readable.

**📌 Verified fact used:** REs shall share / make accessible to the borrowers, through appropriate channels, a statement at the end of each quarter which shall at the minimum, enumerate the principal and interest recovered till date, EMI amount, number of EMIs left and annualized rate of interest / Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for the entire tenor of the loan.
